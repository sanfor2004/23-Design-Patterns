This is a publishing sequence, not a schedule already applied. Choose times from your audience's activity; no audience analytics were available. Record timestamps with a timezone in [metrics.csv](metrics.csv). Do not publish the entire pack to the same audience at once.

X-02 is a finished alternative announcement, not an instruction to repeat Day 1. All post IDs map to copy in [posts.md](posts.md).

Before Day 1, capture available baseline traffic, check the repository link, and upload the [GitHub preview](images/github-social-preview.png) through repository Settings. Suggested About text and topics are in the [SEO tracker](keyword-tracking.md). The local campaign does not apply those settings.

After publication, record each post URL and its 24-hour and seven-day observations. Review helpful replies, outbound clicks, and comparable engagement rates. Describe results only after measurements exist. Use feedback to improve the actual explanation before expanding distribution.
